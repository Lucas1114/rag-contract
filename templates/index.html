Twenty-eight questions, and no twenty-ninth

This service answers a fixed set over six IETF RFCs. There is no free-text input: the questions below are the whole surface, which is what lets every answer replay a committed draft and rerun the grounding check with no model on the request path.

Each answer is a list of claims, and each claim names the passage that is supposed to support it. A check decides, per claim, whether that passage actually does — and the claims that fail are shown to you with the rule that dropped them rather than quietly deleted.

Four outcomes are possible: grounded every claim held, partial some were withdrawn, unsupported none survived and the service refuses, and no_context nothing was retrieved at all. The last eight questions below are about things the corpus does not contain, and they are here to make the refusals visible.
